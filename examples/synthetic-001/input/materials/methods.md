# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT AN OFFICIAL CASE (invented material)

## Measurement note (authoritative source statement for this fixture)

H9c2 cardiomyoblasts were exposed for 24 h to normoxia (control), 1% O2 (hypoxia), or 1% O2 after HIF-1A knockdown (hypoxia+siHIF1A).
Cell viability was measured at 24 h with three biological replicates per condition.
Each bar in the example figure is the mean of the three replicates; the error bars are one standard deviation (SD) around that mean.
Mitochondrial ROS in the example time course are group means and are reported as fold change relative to the 0 h normoxia sample.
The source release reports that viability in hypoxia differs from the control; it does not state a p-value for that comparison.
Therefore the example marks the reported comparison without claiming a p-value.
The draft caption supplied with this fixture is out of date and is not the source of truth.
Units: viability in percent (%), mitochondrial ROS as fold change, time in hours (h).
